WHAT IT IS

Switch To YouTube is a small Flask web app. You sign in to Spotify and YouTube Music, pick your liked songs and the playlists you want, and it rebuilds them on YouTube Music.

  1. Sign in to both services.
  2. Choose your liked songs and/or playlists.
  3. For each song, the app searches YouTube Music and picks the closest match by title, artist, and length.
  4. Liked songs get liked on YouTube Music, and each playlist is recreated with the same name.
  5. At the end, it shows how many songs were found and lists any it couldn't match.

STACK

PYTHON, FLASK, SPOTIFY API (SPOTIPY), YOUTUBE MUSIC API (YTMUSICAPI), DOCKER

WHY I MADE IT

Every tool I could find for moving playlists between services was paid. Moving playlists you already own shouldn't cost money, so I built a free, open-source option anyone can use.
