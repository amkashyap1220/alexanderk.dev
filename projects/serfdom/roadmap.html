Now forging · Phase 12

Dungeon tiers and the endgame

In progress

Levels 1 to 20 go quickly. The long game is harder dungeons, stat potions and rare drops. This phase builds the first rungs of that ladder.

  • The Goblin Stronghold, levels 9–12
  • Falling boulders, ambush holes, arrow traps
  • Cover, sludge and burning floors
  • Slingers, skulkers, bombers and shamans
  • Boss: the Goblin Warchief
  • Stat potions and the potion rack
  • The Forgotten Crypt, levels 12–15
  • Ghosts, necromancers, grave knights
  • Cursed runes and spirit braziers
