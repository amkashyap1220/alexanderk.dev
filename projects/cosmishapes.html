About the game

I made CosmiShapes in Unity with two classmates for our software engineering capstone in high school, in spring 2018.

Shapes attack you level by level, and you shoot your way through: hit the point goal before time runs out to unlock the next level. Between levels, you spend your points in a shop to upgrade your health, speed, and attacks. The last level is a boss fight with waves of enemies.

Built with Unity and C#.
