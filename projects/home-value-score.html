Home Value Score

Best-value zip codes in the Chicago metro

★ -- -- commits -- updated --

Every Chicago-area zip code scored on schools, safety, commute, walkability, home age and price or rent. Readers set their own weights and targets, alone or with a partner, and a map shows their best-value areas next to the places Niche recommends.

In short

  • Method set first. The hypothesis, definitions and pass/fail rule were committed before any price data was downloaded. Later changes are logged with a reason.
  • Data from 10+ public sources, joined at the zip level: Redfin, Census ACS and TIGER files, Illinois and Indiana school results, FBI crime data, the EPA walkability index, EIA gas prices and Niche rankings.
  • Result: with my weights, none of Niche's Illinois top 20 make my top 20 value areas for buying. The 7 that overlap are all Niche's Indiana picks, and the Illinois ones are places like Woodstock, Sycamore, Grayslake and Joliet's east side. Try your own in the write-up.
